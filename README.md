## 中文

一份关于美国国债的机制核查:**$40 万亿之后,债务会以什么形态解决,以及怎样才算看到了它在发生。**

本文不预测崩盘日期。它做三件事:

1. **把口径钉死** —— 总债务 ≠ 公众持有 ≠ 可流通 ≠ 债务上限口径;毛利息 ≠ 净利息;联邦财年赤字 ≠ general government 赤字。绝大多数关于美债的争论,是两个人拿着不同口径的数字在吵。
2. **把机制拆开** —— 高债务在历史上怎么下来的(1946-1980 的 -83pp 里,真实增长只贡献 12pp,金融抑制贡献 36pp);当代的复刻版缺哪个零件(负实际利率);已经造好的部分是什么(GENIUS Act 强制短债需求、eSLR 再校准、Fed 恢复买 bills、财政部长端回购);以及为什么这台机器**已就位但未点火**。
3. **把判据预注册** —— 六个机械触发器(T1–T6),每个钉死判定源、做过出生日回填校验(出生即触发的指标没有预警能力),**触发后不得回改**。

### 页面里有什么

- **§一–§二 债务本体与财政轨迹**:$40.1T 的四个口径、期限结构(bills 占可流通 22.2%、12 个月内到期 33%)、持有人的边际轮换、拍卖健康度的"价格出清 vs 数量拒绝"裁决;净利息 / 收入从 7.4% 升到 20.8% 的一张一手数据自制图。
- **§三–§四 美元与石油美元**:2025 年的贬值与 2026 年的企稳如何并存;「美沙 50 年石油美元协议 2024 年到期」为什么是**虚构**(不存在可到期的协议),以及真实的侵蚀有多慢。
- **§五–§六之二 历史分类学与「金融抑制 2.0」**:原版剧本怎么收税、债权人为什么 30 年跑不掉、以及为什么"学会自卫"之后复刻的是**功能**不是**机制**;附带回答两个常见追问 —— 美股十年牛市为什么化解不了债务、政府有巨额资产为什么不变卖。
- **§七 危机形态与三个断点**:不是拍卖失败,是价格出清点上移 + 财政干预边际失效 + 通胀预期脱锚;含一次**实时核验**——把"全球债市抛售风暴"的头条放进 1990 年以来的历史分位里,结论是速度普通、水平纪录。
- **§八 条件窗口与触发器台账**:为什么"X 年爆发"没有预测力;三情景与它们的概率(主观,附校准声明);T1–T6 的当期读数。
- **§九–§十 已知缺口与来源分级**:数据缺口显式登记不掩盖;每个数字的来源等级与引用纪律。

### 方法纪律(与内容同等重要)

- **来源分级**:✅ 一手官方接口/文档 · 🔶 一手 + 本文自算 · ⚠ 单一来源 · ❔ 公认史实未过三票 · ❌ 已证伪。**⚠ 项不得脱掉标记单独引用**,本文中它们只用于画窗口、不用于下判定。

## English

A mechanism-level audit of US federal debt: **after $40 trillion, in what form does the debt get resolved — and what would it look like to actually see that happening.**

The report does not predict a crash date. It does three things:

1. **Pins down the definitions.** Total public debt ≠ debt held by the public ≠ marketable debt ≠ the debt-limit measure; gross interest ≠ net interest; the federal fiscal-year deficit ≠ the general-government deficit. Most arguments about US debt are two people shouting numbers from different measures.
2. **Takes the mechanism apart.** How high debt has historically come down (of the -83pp from 1946 to 1974, real growth contributed only 12pp; financial repression contributed 36pp); which part the modern replica is missing (a negative real policy rate); which parts are already built (the GENIUS Act's mandated short-bill demand, the eSLR recalibration, the Fed's return to buying bills, the Treasury's long-end buybacks); and why the machine is **assembled but not ignited**.
3. **Pre-registers the criteria.** Six mechanical triggers (T1–T6), each with a fixed adjudication source, each back-filled with its reading on the day it was registered (an indicator that triggers on its birthday has no early-warning value), and **never revised after it fires**.

### What is in it

- **§1–§2 — the debt itself and the fiscal path**: four measures of "$40 trillion", the maturity structure (bills at 22.2% of marketable, 33% maturing within 12 months), the rotation in the marginal buyer, and the ruling that auctions clear on *price*, not by *rejecting quantity*; plus a chart, built from primary API data, of net interest as a share of receipts rising from 7.4% to 20.8%.
- **§3–§4 — the dollar and the petrodollar**: how a 2025 devaluation and a 2026 stabilisation coexist; why "the 50-year US-Saudi petrodollar agreement expired in 2024" is **fiction** (there is no agreement that could expire); and how slow the real erosion actually is.
- **§5–§6b — historical taxonomy and "Financial Repression 2.0"**: how the original playbook collected its tax, why creditors could not escape for 30 years, and why — now that they have learned to defend themselves — what is being replicated is the **function**, not the **mechanism**. Includes two common objections answered in full: why a decade-long equity bull market cannot resolve the debt, and why the government does not simply sell assets.
- **§7 — the shape of a crisis, and three breakpoints**: not a failed auction, but a rising clearing price + fiscal intervention losing traction + inflation expectations un-anchoring. Includes a real-time test: the "global bond rout" headline placed inside the distribution of every 5-day move since 1990 — the speed was ordinary, the levels were records.
- **§8 — conditional windows and the trigger ledger**: why "it blows up in X years" has no predictive content; three scenarios with probabilities (subjective, with a calibration disclaimer); current readings for T1–T6.
- **§9–§10 — known gaps and source grading**: data gaps registered explicitly rather than papered over; a grade and a citation rule for every number.

### Method (as important as the content)

- **Source grading**: ✅ primary official API/document · 🔶 primary + own calculation · ⚠ single source · ❔ widely accepted but not adversarially verified · ❌ falsified. **⚠ items must not be quoted with the marker stripped off** — in this report they size a window, they never settle a question.
- **Corrections stay in place.** Where the report was wrong (a growth-rate denominator, a reference-distribution mismatch, a dimensional mismatch, mistaking the holder's loss for the Treasury's gain), the correction is written **at the original spot rather than edited away**. That is not an appendix; it is the method.
- **Triggers are never revised.** Sources and thresholds are fixed at registration and back-filled with that day's reading; no claiming after the fact that something "was a warning".
- **Probabilities are judgements**, labelled as such, with the standing disclaimer that judgements of this kind have historically over-estimated the speed of change.

### Disclaimer

Everything here is conditional scenario research and is **not investment advice**. The report offers no verdict on whether anything will collapse; it offers verifiable facts, the differences between them, and a set of pre-registered, falsifiable conditions.
